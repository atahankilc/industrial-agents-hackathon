# Organisations phase 1: orgs own buildings, listed / unlisted / private, per-building admins

- **Author:** Claude (Opus 5.5 session) · **Date:** 2026-10-28
- **Related:** D-029 (proposed), Q-022, Q-023, Q-024, docs/product/organizations.md, D-015

## Summary
Backend for organisations: an org owns buildings and its people have roles; a building's listing (listed / unlisted /
private) replaces the public flag; editors and owners of the owning org administer a building like the admin. Also
closes a leak where the Guide's neighbours saw buildings the viewer may not list. Plan for phases 2–4 in the new doc.
