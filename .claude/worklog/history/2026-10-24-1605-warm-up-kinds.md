## Summary
The agent can now answer the kit's warm-up questions (questions_basic.jsonl): locate, count, route,
route_step_free, route_with_closure and tour, in case the Sunday benchmark asks them. Each has a regex template,
a solver behind `solve()`, a reply with an `Answer:` line, and an LLM-parser kind for other wordings. Verified from
the question text against every train+dev warm-up answer. Benchmark answers are unchanged.
