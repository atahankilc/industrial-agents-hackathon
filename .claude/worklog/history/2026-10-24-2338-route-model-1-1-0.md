## Summary
Ports the organisers' kit 1.1.0 route model (archive/initial-repo/starter_agent/building_map.py) into
`core/building_map.py` unchanged and makes it the only route model, so all route answers match the new gold again.
Fixes two rule bugs the new graph exposed (and `solvers_v1.reachable_rooms` now drops narrow door nodes, since walk
edges carry no `via`), updates validators/tests to the 1.1.0 answer format and metres, removes the
obsolete geodesic approximation, regenerates the test submission.
