# Building screen (frontend)

Mode: Operate. Mobile first (390 px), desktop for demos. Visitor (guide-first) and maintainer (whole building + Edit) roles, one app.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Each floor is a quiet architect's plan sheet that proves the Guide's answer; refuses the dark sci-fi twin and the search-box indoor map.
OWN-WORLD: Apple Maps light ground #F5F3EF, white rooms, poché walls #3A3A3C at world thickness, hairline door-swing arcs, room tags in system SF, route #0A84FF, closed #FF3B30 hatch, step-free #34C759, revision clouds for edits; dark mirror.
STORY: Visitor asks, sees the place lift while the floor dims, follows the blue line; maintainer edits and sees a cloud with Δn.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Full-bleed flat plan of one floor; guide line top ("Floor 2 · 24 rooms"); floor tabs right edge; 2D/3D switch top right; Guide sheet bottom (input, mic, camera).
FORM: Drawing Set, #3 of 7, seed 0d225a9b. Raises: pixel-exact linework, guide words, names are controls, focus lifts/rest dims.
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
