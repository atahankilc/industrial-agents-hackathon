## Summary
First version of the app (Phase 4 UI): Vite/React/r3f point cloud in black/white for light and dark mode with
animated emergence, route flow, highlights, closures and lost access, driven by the Guide's events, plus a chat
panel. Backend serves the point cloud per session and enriches route/change events with geometry. Guide gets
persona presets and a deterministic profile safety net.
