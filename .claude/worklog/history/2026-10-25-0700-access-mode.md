with one admin, restricted spaces that leave the viewer's building copy (all Guide tools incl. `solve`) and are
darkened on the plan, remembered preferences (soft route penalties, Guide capture), and moving objects with
live positions and machine-readable missions. The parallel session's unfinished `src/map/` work (city shell) is
NOT in this commit: `src/map/*` are unstaged and the two mixed files are staged without those hunks.
