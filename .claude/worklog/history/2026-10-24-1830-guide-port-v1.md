## Summary
Brings the v1 Guide's strengths into the canonical Guide used by the backend/UI: a place resolver that drops
articles, maps an exact asset type to its room, lists repeated names with what tells them apart and suggests
near-miss names; route fan-out over an ambiguous destination; and a stricter grounding check.
