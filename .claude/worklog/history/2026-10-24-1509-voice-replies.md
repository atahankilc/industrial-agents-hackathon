## Summary
Replies sent to the voice path (ElevenLabs Custom LLM → `/v1/chat/completions`) are now written for speech: the
Guide asks the model for a spoken style on voice turns and a deterministic `speakable()` pass removes markdown,
list markers, ids and symbols. The chat panel (text channel) is unchanged.
