# Organisations phase 2c: Edit mode and imports for org editors

- **Author:** Claude (Opus 5.5 session) · **Date:** 2026-10-29
- **Related:** D-029 (proposed), docs/product/organizations.md (Phase 2)

## Summary
An organisation's editors and owners can now edit their org's buildings in Edit mode. In a verified org they can also
create buildings from floor plans. A saved building belongs to the org, and goes onto the org map it was made from.
Nobody else can reach their drafts.
